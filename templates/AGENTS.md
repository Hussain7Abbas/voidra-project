# Bundled project templates

Extends [root instructions](../AGENTS.md). Templates are plain YAML validated by the public format package. Keep IDs, versions, required format, questions and lifecycle overrides synchronized with their references and evidence.

General 0.1.0 was P04 starter content; the current 1.0.0 file is a P05 review draft. The final general template is version 1.0.0 and requires the explicit P05 product review before shipment. Do not present starter questions or the architecture skill name as approved final content.

Every question must guide later work. Use the supported short-text, long-text, single-choice, multiple-choice and list types; give visible help and valid examples. Preserve the user's ability to leave optional answers open. Inference has a source and remains unconfirmed until the person confirms it.

Keep the sibling CLAUDE.md as the root-compatible instruction pointer.
